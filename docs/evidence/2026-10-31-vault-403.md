`POST /v3/vault/setup-tokens` (payment_source.paypal, usage_type MERCHANT; `web/scripts/vault-setup.mts`) against https://api-m.sandbox.paypal.com returned
`403 NOT_AUTHORIZED` "Authorization failed due to insufficient permissions." four times:

| When (UTC) | Dashboard state | PayPal debug_id |
|---|---|---|
| ~10:10 | Vault not enabled | `f494393d200b7` |
| ~12:20 | "Save payment methods" + "PayPal and Venmo" toggled on, saved | `f19265687d35b` |

After the toggles the OAuth token gained `https://uri.paypal.com/services/billing-agreements` but still had no Vault v3 payment-tokens scope.
PayPal's guide notes reference transactions need account approval for billing agreements.
Decision (four-voice council): holds use per-order buyer approval instead (Orders v2 `payment_source.paypal`, `PAYER_ACTION_REQUIRED` -> buyer approves in the sandbox -> authorize), proven live in `2026-10-31-hosted-simulated.md`.
